Can Waypoint connect to my school?

Yes, if your school uses Canvas and lets students make an access token. Any calendar file (.ics) works too.

Does Waypoint read Home Access Center?

No. HAC has no public way to share grades, so you type them in.

Who can see my plan?

Only you. You can download everything or delete your account in Settings.

Is Waypoint for younger students?

Sign-up is for ages 13 and up.

How long does the Waypoint demo last?

24 hours. It opens a made-up student's week, then it is deleted.
